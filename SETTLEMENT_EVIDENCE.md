## Privacy boundary

These transaction hashes are public blockchain data. This repository does not publish customer identities, private account records, API credentials, database credentials, wallet private material or operational fraud controls to prove settlement.

The gateway's production-derived settlement-verification logic is documented and tested elsewhere in this repository. The hashes above provide public-chain evidence of real BSC payment usage without duplicating UQX contract evidence or exposing private customer data.
